Toddle Tweaks privacy policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Toddle Tweaks is a browser extension that improves the Toddle learning platform (web.toddleapp.com). It has no servers of its own and collects nothing for its developer.

What the extension reads

While you use Toddle, the extension reads information Toddle already shows you, so it can display it differently: your classes, assignments and due dates, class announcements, teacher and classmate names and photos, and your own name and profile picture. To do this it calls Toddle's own API using the sign-in session you already have with Toddle.

Where that information goes

  • It stays in your browser. Assignment lists, class lists and page snapshots are cached in your browser's local storage on toddleapp.com so pages load faster.
  • Your settings (which features are on) are saved with your browser's extension storage and may be synced between your own browsers by your browser's sync feature.
  • Nothing is sent to the developer or to any analytics, advertising or tracking service. The extension has no telemetry.
  • The only network requests the extension makes are to Toddle (to read your data, using your session), and to Google's font and image servers (fonts.googleapis.com, fonts.gstatic.com, www.gstatic.com) to load typefaces and decorative banner images. Those requests, like any web request, include your IP address and browser details, and are covered by Google's privacy policy.

Changes made in Toddle

The extension only changes something in your Toddle account when you ask it to. The one such feature, “Mark all as done in Toddle”, marks past items that don't take a submission as done, exactly like Toddle's own “Mark as done” button, and asks you to confirm first.

Blocking

By default the extension blocks third-party analytics and advertising trackers that Toddle's pages load. This only reduces data sent to those services.

Removing your data

Use Settings → Your data → Clear cache, or simply uninstall the extension. Clearing your browser's site data for toddleapp.com also removes every cached item.

Children's privacy

The extension is used by students. It does not collect, transmit or sell any personal information, so no data from children is ever received by the developer.

Contact

Questions? Contact the developer through the extension's store page.

Toddle Tweaks is an independent project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Toddle or Google.
